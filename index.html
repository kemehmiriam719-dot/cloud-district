FAQs

What products can I find at Cloud District?

Cloud District features a selection of vape products across different categories, including disposable vapes, vape kits, pods, e-liquids and vaporizers. You can also explore our collection by brand to find products that interest you.

What brands are available at Cloud District?

Our collection includes brands such as Alibarbar, Lost Mary, HQD, IGET, Elf Bar, Geek Bar, Vaporesso, OXVA and RELX. Available products may change as our collection is updated.

How do I place an order?

Browse the store and add the products you want to your shopping cart. Once your cart reaches the minimum order value of $200, you can select ORDER NOW and continue your order through WhatsApp.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes. Cloud District has a minimum order value of $200. Orders below $200 cannot proceed to the WhatsApp ordering stage. Your cart will show you exactly how much more you need to add.

How do I pay for my order?

Cloud District does not process payments directly through the website. After meeting the minimum order requirement, you will continue through our contact page where you can discuss your order and payment arrangements with Cloud District.

Can I contact Cloud District before ordering?

Yes. If you have questions about a product, your order or the available options, you can contact Cloud District through WhatsApp for assistance.

How can I explore products from a specific brand?

You can use the Shop by Brand section on the homepage or the brand filters on the Shop page. Select a brand to view the products currently available from that collection.

Are new products added to Cloud District?

Yes. Our Latest Products section highlights products that have recently been added to the Cloud District collection. Check back regularly to discover new additions.

How can I get help with my order?

For questions about an order, product availability or the ordering process, contact Cloud District through WhatsApp and our team can assist you.
